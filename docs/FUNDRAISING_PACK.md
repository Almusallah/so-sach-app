# Sổ Sạch — fundraising pack

Six files, three documents, one sequence. Regenerate any of them by running the
matching script in this folder (`python3 make_deck_prenda.py`, `make_deck_postnda.py`,
`make_nda.py`). Shared brand/layout lives in `deck_kit.py`.

## The sequence

1. **Send the teaser cold.** `SoSach_Teaser_PreNDA_EN.pdf` (or `_IT`). 8 slides.
   Contains nothing confidential — every claim on it is verifiable by the reader
   without your help, and the last slide tells them exactly what signing buys.
2. **They ask for more.** Send `SoSach_NDA_IT.pdf` — the Italian text is the one
   that binds. `SoSach_NDA_EN.pdf` is a courtesy translation and says so on its
   face; send it alongside, never instead.
3. **Signed.** Send `SoSach_Deck_PostNDA_EN.pdf` (or `_IT`). 16 slides, every
   page banded CONFIDENTIAL so a printed copy can never be mistaken for the teaser.

## What is deliberately held back until step 3

Unit economics and COGS · ARPU, CAC, churn and payback · the 30% agent
rev-share and the uncomfortable 248,000 VND/month figure at pilot scale · the
Điểm Sổ Sạch methodology · the provenance model · how the Zalo barrier was
actually cleared · the year-by-year model · the risks and the kill criteria ·
use of funds.

The teaser names these as headings on its final slide. That is the point: it
tells a fund precisely what it is missing, which is what makes signing worth
their lawyer's twenty minutes.

## The NDA — decisions taken, so they aren't re-litigated

- **Discloser is Yuri Frassi personally**, not Officine Gặp. One signature, no
  company seal to obtain in Vietnam.
- **Officine Gặp is a third-party beneficiary under art. 1411 c.c.** (art. 15).
  This closes the gap the choice above opens: the confidential material is the
  company's asset, so without this clause the company would have no standing to
  enforce an agreement it never signed. With it, the company can sue in its own
  name.
- **Unilateral.** Only Yuri discloses. Upgrade to mutual only if a fund asks, or
  when term-sheet talks start and they begin sharing their own material.
- **Three-year term — but trade secrets outlive it** (art. 10). Letting
  trade-secret protection expire with the contract would be an own goal, since
  art. 98 CPI protects them independently and for longer.
- **No penalty clause, no non-compete, no non-solicit.** All three get struck by
  a fund's counsel and cost two weeks. This document is built to be signed, not
  to be won. A harder version is a small edit away if a specific counterparty
  warrants it.
- **Residual-knowledge carve-out** (art. 8), narrowly drawn: unaided memory only,
  no documents, no figures, no partner lists, no licence.
- **Exclusive forum: Milan**, with the art. 1341/1342 specific-approval block on
  the signature page. Without that second signature the exclusive forum clause
  is unenforceable — do not let a counterparty return the document with only one.

## Before you send the NDA to anyone

**Have an Italian lawyer read it.** It was drafted carefully and cites the right
provisions, but it has not been reviewed by anyone admitted to practise, and a
confidentiality agreement is the one document in a fundraise you cannot fix
after the fact. Budget an hour of an avvocato's time; the clauses most worth
their attention are art. 8 (residuals), art. 10 (survival) and art. 15
(third-party beneficiary), since those three are where the drafting is doing
real work rather than reciting boilerplate.

Blanks to fill before sending: Yuri's place and date of birth, codice fiscale
and residence; the Recipient's details are left for them.
